
Wizard spells
=============

Frog
  Curse a target into the form of a frog. Only a hug or damage can cure them. Range: Touch

Curse of Invisibility
  Curse a target with invisibility until they attack or take damage. Is this even a curse? Range: 5

Dominate Mind
  Compel an unshielded target to serve and protect you. Can be broken by a mindshield. Range: Touch

Meteor Swarm
  -NR- Strike a nearby 7x7 tile area with two to four meteors, generally a terrible idea.

Stop time
  Stops time in a 5x5 area for 10 seconds. Only those who know this spell can move.

Curse of the Ancients
  Force the burdens of history upon one unlucky soul. They'll be forced to move only in full tiles at a time. Cured by holy water. Range: Touch

Curse of Hysteria
  Gives the target one random new Thaven mood. Cured by Psicodine. Range: Touch

Curse of Doom
  Give the target foresight into their own demise, they must complete a task and alter their own timeline in 60 seconds or die. Range: Touch

Summon Bees
  Summons a swarm of bees, they're angry, they're very angry, what did you expect? Range: Self

Interior Redecorating
  Channel nature to convert nearby floors, walls, windows, and doors into something a little more organic. Gnome and garden approved! Range: 4

New Perspective
  Make a target dizzy, twisting their view until they take a moment to sit on a chair and recover. Range: 4

Magic Missile
  Fire a slow homing orb at every nearby target, hits knock them down temporarily. Range: 4

Curse of the Snail
  Force a target to crawl like a snail, they'll even leave a slippery trail! Range: 3

Curse of Noir
  Give a target Noir Vision, forcing them to see the world in black, white and red until they have a drink at the bar. Range: 4

Crowd Swap
  Shuffle the locations of all nearby living creatures within 5 tiles, who knows where you'll end up!

Static Electricity
  Curse a target to shock anyone who touches them. Cured by eating insulated gloves. Range: Touch

Gravity Shift
  Turns off gravity in a localized area, leaving too quickly will cause you to fall flat on your face! Range: Self

Tornado
  Send forth a vortex of wind to pull and spin everything nearby. Travels in a line.

Grease
  Spread slippery, flammable welding fuel over a 4x4 area. Range: 5

Sleeping Cloud
  Releases a gas that puts everyone to sleep! Conjures a magical grenade to your hand.

Combust
  Releases a gas that quickly detonates explosively. Conjures a magical grenade to your hand.

Magical Sauna
  Detonates into a sauna of magical steam! Conjures a magical grenade to your hand.

-NR- Stink Cloud
  -NR- Releases a choking cloud of ammonia. Conjures a magical grenade to your hand.

Trash Golem
  -NR- Animate up to ten loose items in a 3x3 area centered on you into an obedient golem.

Asphyxiate
  Consume all oxygen in a 3x3 area for 20 seconds. Range: 5

Cone of Hellfire
  Release a cone of hellfire, also probably run. Range: 2

Cone of Cold
  -NR- Freeze targets for four seconds, throw them back, and leave ice in their wake. Deals ten cold damage across an area three tiles long and three tiles wide. Range: 3

Chain Lightning
  Unleash the power of lightning! Strikes a living target for 40 shock damage. Chains to the nearest new target within 3 tiles twice and deals decreased damage. Range: 5

Dimension Door
  Create a two way portal to somewhere else, where? Not sure, try it and find out! Remains until cancelled. May have side effects. Range: Touch

Arcane Barrage
  -NR- Raise 15 homing orbs in groups of three that divide among nearby targets, dealing blunt and stamina damage.

Lightning Barrage
  -NR- Raise 15 lightning balls in groups of three. Each deals 4 shock damage and can bounce twice between nearby people.

Barrage upgrade tiers
=====================

Arcane Flurry
  -NR- Raise 30 homing orbs in groups of three that divide among nearby targets, dealing blunt and stamina damage.

Arcane Storm
  -NR- Raise 45 homing orbs in groups of three that divide among nearby targets, dealing blunt and stamina damage.

Lightning Flurry
  -NR- Raise 30 lightning balls in groups of three. Each deals 4 shock damage and can bounce twice between nearby people.

Lightning Storm
  -NR- Raise 45 lightning balls in groups of three. Each deals 4 shock damage and can bounce twice between nearby people.

Cantrips
========

Summon Duck
  -NR-Summon one ordinary duck nearby.

Summon Bee
  -NR-Summon one non-hostile bee nearby.

Magical Ale
  A conjured bottle of booze, glows slightly when shaken.

Telepathy
  Telapathically send a message directly to someone's mind. Range: 4

Minor Illusion
  Conjure an Illusionary image of a random creature. Range: 4

Speak with Dead
  Hear nearby ghosts for 30 seconds. Can be canceled at any time.

Mage Hand
  Control an ethereal hand that can interact with nearby objects.

Arcane Chalk
  -NR- Summon chalk with one charge. Its writing remains until cleaned.

Mime abilities
==============

Invisible Barrier
  Form a 1x3 Invisible barrier behind you for one minute. Great at blocking entire hallways!

Finger Gun
  Draw or put away your trusty finger gun. Hits Inject a small amount of mute toxin into the target. Recovers 1 bullet every 20 seconds.

Invisible Lasso
  Throw a lasso at your target, a successful hit knocks them down and drags them back towards you.

Related magic items
===================

contract of apprenticeship
  -NR- Bound to the wizard who purchased it. A prospective apprentice can sign with a pen.

wand of random curses
  Fires a projectile that applies a random curse to anyone hit. Results may vary.

Wand of Random Creatures
  Summons a random creature who will consider you friendly, good luck to everyone else. Five Charges.

Staff of Many Guns
  Conjures a random spectral gun that fires in the targeted direction for ten seconds. Results may vary.
